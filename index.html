The HOLYGATE Promise to Indian Kitchens

Understanding the difference in what you bring home to your family.

UNPOLISHED

100% Unpolished Pulses & Lentils

Most commercial dals undergo water, oil, or leather polishing that strips away the outer bran layer rich in dietary fibre, folate, and essential minerals. HOLYGATE pulses (Arhar, Chana, Moong, Urad, Masoor, Rajma) are completely unpolished, preserving authentic taste and natural nutrition.

TRADITIONAL

Chemical-Free Desi Khand

Unlike industrially refined white sugar treated with sulphur dioxide and bone-char clarifiers, HOLYGATE Desi Khand is made from pure cold-pressed sugarcane juice through natural sun-drying and traditional centrifuging, retaining wholesome trace minerals.

COLD SORTEX

Double-Cleaned & Stone-Free

Every batch passes through optical Sortex color-sorters and high-frequency destoning sifters. You receive clean, insect-free, dust-free whole grains ready for cooking straight out of the packet.
